About ElectroFyre

At ElectroFyre, we specialize in bringing warmth, style, and convenience to homes across Canada. Based in the heart of Sarnia, Ontario, we take pride in offering high-quality, home-style electric fireplaces designed to enhance your living space. Our products combine sleek designs with cutting-edge technology, delivering both comfort and energy efficiency to your home.


Our mission is simple: to create cozy, inviting spaces that transform houses into homes. Whether you're looking for a centerpiece for your living room or an elegant addition to your bedroom, ElectroFyre offers the perfect fireplace for every style and need.


Six Reasons Why You Should Shop with Us

  • Canadian-Made Quality: We are proudly based in Sarnia, Ontario, and all our products meet rigorous quality standards.
  • Stylish Designs: Choose from a wide selection of modern, classic, and customizable fireplace styles to suit your taste.
  • Energy Efficiency: Our electric fireplaces are eco-friendly, cost-effective, and designed for maximum energy savings.
  • Customer-Centric Service: Our team is dedicated to ensuring your shopping experience is smooth, informative, and enjoyable.
  • Affordable Pricing: Enjoy premium-quality products without breaking the bank. We offer competitive prices and regular sales.
  • Hassle-Free Installation: All our fireplaces are easy to install, making it simple to bring warmth to your home without the need for complicated renovations.
