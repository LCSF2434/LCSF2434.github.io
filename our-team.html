Our Story

ElectroFyre was born out of a simple yet transformative idea: to reimagine how people experience warmth and comfort in their homes. Founded in 2018 in Sarnia, Ontario, ElectroFyre began as a small, family-run operation with a mission to provide stylish, energy-efficient alternatives to traditional fireplaces. It all started when the company's founder, Alex Robertson, noticed a gap in the market for fireplaces that combined modern aesthetics with functionality. Inspired by the growing need for eco-friendly home solutions, Alex assembled a team of designers, engineers, and craftsmen to create a product that would redefine the industry. The result was ElectroFyre's first line of electric fireplaces, which quickly gained popularity for their realistic flames and sleek designs. In just a few short years, ElectroFyre evolved from a local startup to one of Canada's leading electric fireplace companies. Our dedication to innovation has led to the development of groundbreaking technologies, such as the RealGlow™ Flame Technology, which delivers an authentic fireplace experience without the need for wood, gas, or harmful emissions. Today, ElectroFyre remains headquartered in Sarnia, Ontario, where our passionate team continues to design and produce high-quality electric fireplaces. Despite our rapid growth, we've stayed true to our roots, focusing on craftsmanship, sustainability, and exceptional customer service. From humble beginnings to industry leader, ElectroFyre's journey has always been fueled by a desire to bring warmth, style, and innovation into homes across Canada. And we're just getting started.


ElectroFyre Facts!

  • Our team has collectively designed over 500 fireplaces, bringing warmth and style to homes across Canada!
  • ElectroFyre holds a patent for its unique RealGlow™ Flame Technology, which creates the most realistic electric fireplace flames on the market.
  • All of our fireplaces are designed and assembled in Sarnia, Ontario, supporting local artisans and contributing to the Canadian economy.
